# Airepro brand brief

**Name:** Airepro  
**Website / CTA:** https://airepro.in/  
**Internships CTA:** https://airepro.in/view/internships  
**Tagline:** Your future. Our mission.

## Positioning

Airepro is the platform to find top internships and freelance opportunities. We connect students, early-career talent, and freelancers with real work—and help employers find motivated people.

## Audience

Students, early-career professionals, freelancers, and employers seeking talent.

## Voice

Clear, encouraging, and opportunity-focused. Practical and specific—never vague “hustle” fluff. Do not invent Meta/Facebook “verification” how-tos or unrelated tech tutorials unless the topic angle explicitly asks for that.

## Messaging pillars (creative + caption)

Use these as canonical ingredients for Facebook visual posts and creatives:

1. **Hook** — opportunity-first (e.g. Find Your Dream Internship)
2. **Brand line** — Start Building Your Career Today with Airepro
3. **Body** — Explore verified internships and opportunities that help you learn, grow and take the next step in your career.
4. **Benefit grid (4):**
   - Verified Internships
   - Opportunities Across Multiple Domains
   - Build Real-World Experience
   - Grow Your Skills & Career
5. **CTA banner** — Explore Internships Now → `airepro.in/view/internships`
6. **Footer chips:** For Students & Freshers · Remote & On-site Opportunities · Opportunities That Matter

## Facebook visual caption format (required)

When posting with a branded creative image, keep the **caption to exactly 3 short lines**:

1. Hook (optional single emoji)
2. Brand line naming **Airepro**
3. `Visit: https://airepro.in` (or `https://airepro.in/view/internships`)

Example:

```text
Find Your Dream Internship 🚀
Start Building Your Career Today with Airepro
Visit: https://airepro.in
```

Do **not** write long essay-style Facebook captions in visual mode.

## Hard rules for every post

1. Always name **Airepro** in the copy.
2. Include **https://airepro.in/** as a clear CTA on Facebook, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. On X/Twitter, include the URL when character budget allows; otherwise still make the post clearly about Airepro. Prefer **https://airepro.in/view/internships** for internship-focused creatives.
3. Do not invent unrelated products, apps, or services.
4. Stay on internships, freelance work, and career opportunities aligned with Airepro.
5. The CLI topic is an **angle within this brand** (e.g. “remote internships for students”), not a standalone subject.
